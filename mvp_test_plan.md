# MVP user test plan: SIP Pause Guide

Goal: see whether real first-time SIP investors can use the tool on a real task, understand the options, and pick a lighter option when it fits. Test with at least 3 people (aim for 5) from the target segment. Return to people from your survey or interviews where you can.

## 1. Who
Salaried or early-career first-time SIP investors, SIP started in the last 12 months. At least one person who has really thought about pausing (for example survey rows 24, 25, 26, 33 or the interviewees who stopped or nearly paused). Label them T1, T2, T3. No names anywhere.

## 2. Before you start (2 minutes)
- Say: "This is a prototype of a feature that could live in Groww. It is not connected to your account. It is not investment advice. Please do not type personal or account details. You can stop any time."
- Ask permission to take notes. Do not record names or numbers.

## 3. The task (their own situation first)
Ask: "Think about the last time you wanted to pause, reduce or stop your SIP, or a time you might. Imagine you are in that moment. Use the tool to decide what to do. Please speak your thoughts out loud."
If they have no real moment, use ONE scenario card:
- Card A (cash): "It is the 25th. Rent and a family expense are due and your SIP debit is in 5 days."
- Card B (returns): "You open your SIP and see the value is below what you invested after 4 months."
- Card C (confusion): "Money was debited yesterday but you can't see your units."
Do not explain the screens. Do not help unless they are stuck for more than a minute (then note it).

## 4. What to observe and write down (per person)
| Item | How to record |
|---|---|
| Did they type or tap a chip? | Typed / chip |
| What they typed (summary only, no personal details) | Short note |
| Did the "What we understood" sentence match? | Yes / Partly / No |
| Did they change the reason? | Yes / No |
| Which option did they pick? | Skip / Reduce / Pause / Stop / None |
| Minutes from start to choice | Number |
| Where did they hesitate or get lost? | Short note |
| Did anything feel pushy, scary, or like advice? | Yes / No, and where |

## 5. Questions after the task (5 minutes)
1. In your own words, what does "Skip this month" do? What happens to your units? (Comprehension check. Note right or wrong.)
2. Before today, did you know you could skip or reduce an instalment on Groww? (Awareness: Yes / No / Not sure)
3. Would you have stopped your SIP without this? What would you have done instead?
4. How much did you trust the result? 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely). Why?
5. What was missing or confusing? What would you change first?
6. Would you use this inside the app? (Yes / Maybe / No)

## 7. What to report in the deck
- N people, how they were recruited, how many were from the target segment. Say it is a small sample.

Do not claim the tool improves retention. Say what was observed.
